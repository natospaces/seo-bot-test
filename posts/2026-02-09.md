### The Importance of Reliable Excel Models in Collaborative Decision-Making

Engineers often build complex data pipelines to support decisions, but a common mistake occurs when these teams overlook the importance of Excel models for non-technical leaders. When engineers focus exclusively on sophisticated analytics without involving key stakeholders, misalignment happens. This gap can lead to distrust or confusion about the insights provided. Non-technical leaders rely on Excel because it presents data simply and intuitively, facilitating quick decisions. When engineers disregard this tool, they risk alienating those who need to act on the data.

Moreover, teams sometimes present results from advanced analytics without full context, which can lead to misguided decisions. If leaders don’t fully understand the data pipeline’s intricacies, they may misinterpret the results or take actions based on flawed assumptions. Open communication can bridge this divide, ensuring everyone is on the same page.

While modern AI tools can enhance predictive analytics or streamline data processing, they can also complicate matters if leaders don't grasp their implications. The challenge remains to maintain fundamental models, like Excel, that provide clear insights. Using these alongside new technologies can help build trust and ensure better decision-making across the organization.
