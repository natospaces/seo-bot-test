### Common Pitfalls in Ensuring Data Reliability in Microsoft Fabric

Data reliability is critical when engineers and non-technical leadership collaborate in Microsoft Fabric. A prevalent issue arises when engineers overly trust the data sources without thorough validation. When engineers assume data integrity, they may inadvertently introduce flawed data into analytical models. This inconsistency leads to uninformed decisions by leadership, risking project timelines and budgets.

In many cases, leaders depend on engineering reports for strategic decisions. If engineers fail to validate data sources or implement rigorous testing processes, executives may act on inaccurate reports. This can cause misalignment between technical teams and business goals, ultimately undermining trust in data-driven initiatives.

Moreover, when teams employ rapid deployment cycles, the pressure to produce can lead to overlooked data quality checks. As engineers rush to meet deadlines, they may cut corners on verifying the accuracy of the data processed through Microsoft Fabric. The result is often a cascade of erroneous insights, causing confusion in board meetings and hampering effective decision-making.

While modern AI tools can streamline data processing, they can exacerbate these issues if the underlying data quality isn’t ensured. Automation should support rigorous data validation processes, but it cannot replace the foundational need for reliable, verified data.
