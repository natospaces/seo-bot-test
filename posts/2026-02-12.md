### Evaluating Excel-Based Pipelines for Data Engineering Workflows

Excel-based pipelines can be beneficial for simple data manipulation tasks, especially in organizations with non-technical leadership. Non-technical teams often prefer Excel for its familiarity, allowing for quick decision-making. However, reliance on Excel can lead to significant issues. For example, engineers may unintentionally introduce errors during data entry, which non-technical leaders may overlook, causing misinformation to propagate throughout decision-making processes. 

Moreover, Excel’s lack of version control makes it challenging to track changes. When multiple users collaborate, mistakes can occur due to conflicting edits. These errors can result in misaligned business strategies, affecting overall project outcomes.

Engineers also struggle with managing dependencies and scaling pipelines built in Excel. As complexity increases, the limitations become more apparent, leading to potential project failures if not addressed promptly. Non-technical leaders may misunderstand these limitations and push for immediate results, exacerbating the impact of hastily made decisions.

While newer AI tools can automate data processes and potentially reduce human errors, they do not eliminate the foundational issues associated with Excel files. AI can enhance pipeline reliability, but if Excel-based workflows remain at the core, risks still exist. Proper training and alternative tech solutions might better align technical work with business objectives.
