### Best Practices to Counter Unreliable Dashboards in Data Reporting

Unreliable dashboards can significantly hamper decision-making for leadership, especially when non-technical teams interpret data blindly. Data engineers often face challenges when dashboards do not reflect the most current data or accurate metrics, leading to misguided strategies. When engineers don’t understand the business context, they may overlook critical metrics, leaving leaders unaware of essential issues. This lack of alignment can cause projects to go off track.

To prevent these issues, data engineers should establish a habit of routine cross-functional check-ins. By meeting regularly with stakeholders, engineers can clarify business objectives and ensure that dashboard metrics are aligned with leadership's decision-making needs. This collaboration also allows leaders to express their requirements and concerns, which can enhance the accuracy of the dashboard's insights.

As teams increasingly adopt automation and AI tools, there's a risk of amplifying data accuracy issues. Without proper oversight, automated reporting can generate misleading outputs. However, when used thoughtfully, AI can also improve data validation processes, offering better assurance in dashboard reliability. Balancing automation with human oversight ensures that data remains trustworthy and actionable for effective decision-making.
