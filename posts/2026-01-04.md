### Ensuring Data Reliability in Microsoft Fabric for Effective Decision-Making

Data engineers often face significant challenges in ensuring data reliability when collaborating with non-technical leadership. Inconsistent data quality can lead to misguided decisions, causing frustration and mistrust between teams. Engineers must create a clear protocol for data validation before sharing insights with leadership. Implementing routine checks at each stage of the data pipeline is essential. This ensures the data is not only accurate but also consistent, allowing leaders to make informed decisions with confidence.

When leadership demands quick insights, engineers might rush data processes, resulting in incomplete or erroneous outputs. Establishing a culture that prioritizes thorough validation, even in the face of tight deadlines, mitigates these risks. Regularly communicating the importance of data checks to leadership helps them understand the value of accuracy over speed.

AI tools can assist in automating data validation, reducing manual workload and potential human error. However, reliance on these tools without human oversight can exacerbate issues if they yield inaccurate data. Thus, engineers should balance AI capabilities with robust validation practices to uphold data reliability effectively.
